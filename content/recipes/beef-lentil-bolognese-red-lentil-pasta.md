### 40% red lentil fresh pasta (4 servings; about 320 kcal, 17.5 g protein, 4.5 g fiber each)
- 120 g red lentil flour (or dried red lentils blitzed and sifted)
- 180 g semolina or 00 flour
- 3 large eggs
- 1 tbsp water, only if dry

### Side
- 100 g broccoli per person
- 1 tsp olive oil per person
- Lemon

## Method

1. Mix flours, make a well, add eggs; knead 8–10 minutes until smooth.
2. Wrap and rest 30 minutes so the lentil flour hydrates.
3. Roll slightly thicker than usual; cut wide (pappardelle or tagliatelle).
4. Cook 2–3 minutes in salted water.
5. Roast the broccoli with the olive oil and finish with lemon (about 75 kcal, 3.3 g fiber per person).
6. Serve the pasta with one serving of bolognese and the broccoli.

## Notes

- Don't go above about 40% lentil flour without adding 1 tbsp vital wheat gluten; the dough tears.
- Freeze extra pasta as nests and cook from frozen.
