## Ingredients

### Kofta
- 280 g 75/25 ground beef
- 2 cups cooked green lentils, patted dry
- 1 small onion, grated and squeezed dry
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- ¼ cup fresh parsley, finely chopped
- 2 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp ground allspice
- 1 tsp smoked or sweet paprika
- 1½ tsp salt

### Garlic yogurt sauce
- 120 g plain Greek yogurt (2%)
- 1 garlic clove, finely grated
- 1 tsp lemon juice
- ½ tsp dried mint or dill
- 2 tsp extra virgin olive oil, for drizzling

### To serve
- 4 whole-wheat pitas
- 2 cucumbers, diced
- 4 tomatoes, diced
- ½ red onion, thinly sliced
- Lemon and salt for the salad

## Method

1. Stir together the yogurt, grated garlic, lemon juice, mint and a pinch of salt. Chill.
2. Roughly mash the lentils with a fork until about half are broken down.
3. Combine the beef, lentils, onion, garlic, parsley, spices and salt. Knead 1–2 minutes until tacky.
4. Shape into 16 small logs or press onto skewers. Chill 15 minutes.
5. Broil on a rack, grill or pan-fry over medium-high heat, turning, until browned and cooked through (160°F), 10–12 minutes.
6. Toss the cucumber, tomato and red onion with lemon and salt.
7. Warm the pitas. Spoon yogurt onto plates, drizzle with olive oil and paprika, then add kofta, salad and pita.

## Notes

- Grilling on a rack renders some of the beef fat; pan-frying keeps more (about +30 kcal per serving).
- One batch of about 5 cups cooked green lentils (2 cups dry) covers this and the Misir Wot.
