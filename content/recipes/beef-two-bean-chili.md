## Ingredients

### Beans
- 6 oz (170 g) dried kidney beans
- 6 oz (170 g) dried black beans
- 1 tsp salt

### Chili
- 300 g 75/25 ground beef
- 1 large onion, diced
- 2 bell peppers, diced
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 3 tbsp chili powder
- 2 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 2 tbsp tomato paste
- 1 can (28 oz) crushed tomatoes
- 1 can (15.5 oz) corn, drained
- 1½–2 cups bean cooking liquid (or low-sodium broth)
- ½ tsp salt, to taste
- 1 lime, cut in wedges

### Toppings (divided among 5 bowls)
- 60 g shredded Mexican cheese blend
- 60 g plain Greek yogurt (2%)
- 2 green onions, sliced

## Method

1. Rinse both beans and soak together 8 hours or overnight, then drain. (Quick soak: boil 2 minutes, cover and stand 1 hour.)
2. Cover with 2 inches of fresh water, add 1 tsp salt and simmer until both are creamy, 60–90 minutes (Instant Pot: about 10–12 minutes at high pressure, natural release). Kidney beans must be fully cooked, never undercooked. Drain, saving 2 cups of the cooking liquid.
3. Brown the beef in a large pot over medium-high heat, about 6 minutes. Spoon off most of the fat, leaving about 1 tbsp.
4. Add the onion and peppers and cook about 6 minutes, adding garlic for the last minute.
5. Stir in the chili powder, cumin, paprika, oregano and tomato paste. Toast 1–2 minutes.
6. Add the beans, crushed tomatoes, 1½ cups bean liquid and salt. Simmer partly covered for 30 minutes, adding more bean liquid if it gets too thick. Stir in the corn for the last 10 minutes. Mash about a cup of beans to thicken.
7. Season, squeeze in lime, and top each bowl with cheese, yogurt and green onion.

## Notes

- Cook the beans before adding them, since tomato acid keeps raw beans from softening.
- Canned corn that isn't no-salt-added adds about 700 mg sodium to the pot.
- Save the extra bean liquid for reheating leftovers.
- Freezes well for up to 3 months; add toppings when serving.
