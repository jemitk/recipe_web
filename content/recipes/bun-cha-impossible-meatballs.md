## Ingredients

### Meatballs
- 1 lb (454 g) Impossible Beef
- ½ lb (227 g) soft tofu, drained and mashed
- 2 stalks lemongrass, minced (or 2 tbsp paste)
- ½ small onion, finely minced and squeezed dry
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 2 tsp fish sauce
- 1 tsp allulose
- 1 tsp black pepper
- 1 tbsp cornstarch
- A little oil for cooking

### Nuoc cham
- ¼ cup fish sauce
- ¼ cup lime juice
- 3 tbsp allulose
- ¾–1 cup warm water
- 2 garlic cloves
- 1–2 Thai chilies

### Quick pickles
- 1½ carrots, thinly sliced
- 200 g radishes (about 1 bunch), thinly sliced
- ⅔ cup rice vinegar
- 1½ tbsp allulose
- ¾ tsp salt

### To serve
- 240 g (about 1⅓ cups) dry jasmine rice, about 1 cup cooked per serving
- 2 cups shelled edamame (½ cup per serving)
- 1 head lettuce
- 1 cucumber
- 2 bell peppers, thinly sliced, for crunch
- Mint, Thai basil, green onion, perilla (optional)

## Method

1. Pickle the carrot and radishes for 30+ minutes.
2. Mix the meatball ingredients; chill 30+ minutes (the tofu makes it soft and moist). Shape into about 20 small flat patties with wet hands.
3. Sear or broil 6–8 minutes, turning once, until charred at the edges.
4. Mix the nuoc cham and add some pickles to it.
5. Cook the jasmine rice.
6. Serve rice, herbs, veg and edamame on the side, and dip everything in the nuoc cham.

## Notes

- Go easy on drinking the nuoc cham; it's high in sodium.
- The pickle amounts include an extra ½ cup for the Thai Mango Chicken Salad. Pickles keep 1–2 weeks.
- Components keep 3–4 days in the fridge.
