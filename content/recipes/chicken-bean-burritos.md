## Ingredients

### Per burrito
- 1 BFree High Protein Carb Savvy wrap
- 50 g cooked chicken tenderloin
- ½ bell pepper, sliced, sautéed in 2 g olive oil
- ¼ can pinto beans, drained
- 15 g shredded Mexican cheese

### On the side
- 2 oz tortilla chips
- ¼ cup salsa

## Method

1. Sauté the pepper in the oil; warm the beans.
2. Fill the wrap with chicken, beans, pepper and cheese; roll tightly.
3. To freeze: wrap each in foil and freeze. Thaw overnight in the fridge, then reheat and serve with chips and salsa.

## Notes

- Sodium runs about 1,200 mg per plate with the chips and salsa.
- Protein and fiber depend on the wrap's label.
- Burritos keep about 3 months frozen.
