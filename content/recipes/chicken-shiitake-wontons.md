## Ingredients

### Wrappers
- 240 g all-purpose flour
- ½ tsp salt
- 110 ml water, room temperature
- 2 tbsp cornstarch, for dusting

### Filling
- 500 g chicken tenderloins, finely minced
- 200 g shiitake mushrooms, finely chopped
- 200 g napa cabbage, finely chopped
- 4 green onions, finely sliced
- 2 tsp fresh ginger, grated
- 4 tsp soy sauce
- 2 tsp Shaoxing wine (or dry sherry)
- 1 tsp toasted sesame oil
- ½ tsp white pepper
- 4 tsp cornstarch

### Chili sauce
- 4 garlic cloves, finely minced
- 1 tsp red chili flakes
- ½ tsp ground Sichuan pepper
- ½ tsp Chinese five spice
- 2 tsp sugar
- 1 tsp chicken bouillon powder
- 4 tsp neutral oil
- 4 tsp chili crisp or Chinese chili oil
- 3 tbsp soy sauce
- 2 tbsp Chinese black vinegar (or rice vinegar)
- 6 tbsp hot water

### Veggies and garnish
- 500 g broccoli, cut into small florets
- 300 g shelled edamame
- 2 green onions, sliced

## Method

1. Mix the flour and salt, gradually add water and knead about 8 minutes into a smooth, firm dough. Cover and rest 30 minutes.
2. Salt the cabbage, wait 5 minutes and squeeze out the water. Mix with the remaining filling ingredients, stirring hard in one direction until sticky. Chill.
3. Roll the dough paper-thin (about 1 mm) and cut into 9 cm (3.5 inch) squares. Dust with cornstarch and keep covered.
4. Fill each with a heaping tablespoon of filling. Wet the edges, fold into a triangle pressing out air, then pinch the side corners together.
5. Put the garlic, chili flakes, Sichuan pepper, five spice, sugar and bouillon in a heatproof bowl. Pour shimmering-hot oil over them, then stir in the chili crisp, soy sauce, vinegar and hot water.
6. Boil the broccoli and edamame 2–3 minutes until just tender. Lift out into bowls.
7. Boil the wontons until they float, plus 1 minute (about 5 minutes fresh). Check one is cooked through.
8. Top the veggies with wontons, sauce and green onion.

## Notes

- Roughly 1,300 mg sodium per serving.
- Halve the chili crisp to bring it to about 600 kcal.
- Freeze uncooked wontons on a tray, then bag them; boil from frozen 7–8 minutes.
