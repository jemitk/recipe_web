## Ingredients

- 600 g raw chicken breast (weighed before cooking)
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 onion, 3 carrots, 3 celery stalks, diced
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 8 cups low-sodium chicken broth
- 1 can (15 oz) cannellini beans, drained and rinsed
- 1 lb frozen peas
- 3–4 big handfuls baby spinach
- Homemade egg noodles (1 egg + 120 g all-purpose flour), fresh or frozen
- 2 bay leaves, 1 tsp dried thyme, pepper
- Juice of ½ lemon
- Dill or parsley to finish

## Method

1. Sauté onion, carrot and celery in the oil for 6–8 minutes; add garlic for 1 minute.
2. Add broth, bay leaves, thyme and whole chicken breasts. Simmer 15–18 minutes until the chicken is cooked through, then shred it.
3. Add beans; simmer 5 minutes. Add the peas for the last 3–4 minutes, then stir in the spinach right before serving, just until wilted. Finish with lemon and herbs; salt to taste.
4. Cook the noodles separately (straight from frozen, no thawing, about 2–4 minutes) and add them per bowl, so they don't go mushy in the leftovers.

## Notes

- Keeps 3–4 days in the fridge. Store noodles separately from the soup.
