## Ingredients

### Lentils
- 2 tbsp butter
- 4 cardamom pods, cracked (or a pinch of ground)
- ½ tsp ground fenugreek (optional)
- 2 large onions, very finely chopped
- 6 garlic cloves, minced
- 2 tbsp fresh ginger, grated
- 3 tbsp berbere spice
- ¼ cup tomato paste
- 3 cups cooked green lentils
- 3 cups water or low-sodium broth
- 1½ tsp salt

### Chicken and sides
- 500 g chicken tenderloins
- 1 tbsp berbere spice
- 2 tsp oil
- 300 g fresh spinach
- 2 garlic cloves, sliced
- 2 cups cooked white rice
- 1 lemon, cut in wedges

## Method

1. Melt the butter with the cardamom and fenugreek over low heat for 2 minutes. Set aside.
2. Cook the onions in a dry pot over medium heat, stirring often, until jammy and starting to color, 8–10 minutes. Add a splash of water if they stick.
3. Add the spiced butter (discard the pods), garlic, ginger, 3 tbsp berbere and tomato paste. Stir 2 minutes until brick-red.
4. Add the lentils, water and salt. Simmer until thick and stewy, about 15 minutes, mashing some lentils against the pot.
5. Meanwhile, toss the chicken with 1 tbsp berbere, the oil and a pinch of salt. Sear 3–4 minutes per side until cooked through. Rest, then slice.
6. In the same skillet, cook the sliced garlic 30 seconds, then wilt the spinach with a pinch of salt, about 2 minutes.
7. Serve the lentils with rice, chicken and spinach, with lemon squeezed over.

## Notes

- Berbere heat varies by brand, so start with less if yours is spicy.
- A spoonful of Greek yogurt adds richness (+20 kcal).
