## Ingredients

### Fish and glaze
- 2 tilapia fillets (about 130 g each), patted dry
- 1 tbsp white miso
- 1 tsp low-sodium soy sauce
- 1 tsp honey or maple syrup
- 1 tsp rice vinegar
- ½ tsp grated ginger

### Bowl
- ¾ cup cooked pearled barley + ¾ cup cooked short-grain rice, mixed (cook separately, since barley takes about 25–30 minutes)
- 300 g broccoli florets
- 1 tsp sesame oil
- Green onion

### Cucumber and edamame salad
- 1 cucumber, thinly sliced
- 1 cup shelled edamame (½ cup per serving)
- Pinch of salt
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar
- ½ tsp allulose
- Sesame seeds

### Optional boost
- ¼ avocado per bowl
- Extra ¼ cup barley-rice per bowl

## Method

1. Brush tilapia with the glaze; bake at 425°F for 8–10 minutes until it flakes, or broil the last 2 minutes for char.
2. Steam the broccoli. Cook the edamame and let it cool.
3. Salt the cucumber for 10 minutes, squeeze out the water, then toss with the edamame, vinegar, allulose and sesame seeds.
4. Build bowls: barley-rice, tilapia, broccoli, cucumber-edamame salad; finish with sesame oil and green onion.

## Notes

- Tilapia is much leaner than salmon, so the optional boost brings the bowl back to about 600 kcal.
