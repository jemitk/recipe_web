## Ingredients

### Salad
- 300 g raw chicken breast (weighed before cooking), grilled or poached and sliced
- 1 ripe mango, sliced (about 200 g flesh)
- 4 cups chopped romaine
- 1 cup shredded red or napa cabbage
- 1 carrot, shredded
- ½ cucumber, sliced
- ½ cup pickled radish and carrot, drained (see Bun Cha recipe for the quick pickles)
- 1 cup shelled edamame, thawed
- Mint, Thai basil, green onion
- 40 g sliced almonds, toasted

### Dressing
- 2 tbsp almond butter
- 2 tbsp rice vinegar
- 1 tbsp sriracha
- 1½ tsp soy sauce
- Juice of ½ lime
- 2–3 tbsp warm water to thin
- Optional: 1 tsp allulose to round out the tang

## Method

1. Whisk the dressing until pourable.
2. Toss romaine, cabbage, carrot, cucumber, edamame and herbs with most of the dressing.
3. Top with chicken, mango and almonds; drizzle the rest.
